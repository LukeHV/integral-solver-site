Working

    Answers are checked by differentiating them; definite integrals are also checked numerically. ln means natural log.

    How to type integrals

    Type maths the way you'd write it. The preview under the box shows how it has been read, so you can check before solving. A trailing dx is optional, log means base 10 (use ln for base e), and ln, log, exp, abs, sqrt and cbrt work with or without brackets.

    Definite integrals

    Choose Definite and fill in the limits. Limits can be numbers, fractions, pi/2, e, ln 2 or inf / -inf (or press ∞). If the integrand blows up inside the interval, the integral is split there and checked for divergence.

    What it can do

    Standard integrals, the reverse chain rule, substitution, integration by parts (including the cyclic kind), partial fractions, completing the square, trig identities and reduction formulas, and trig substitution. Letters other than the variable (like a or k) are treated as constants.
